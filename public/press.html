About Afri OS

Afri OS is the operating system for African business. Founded in 2026 and headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, Afri OS builds the customer relationship management (CRM), enterprise resource planning (ERP), and operational tools that African businesses need — designed for the way they actually work, not as a localized version of Western software.

Afri OS products are mobile-first, support Google single sign-on with one-click Gmail and Calendar integration, ship with default Naira currency and Lagos timezone awareness, and respect Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). The platform is free for solo founders and small teams, with paid tiers for growing businesses.

The Afri OS suite currently includes Afri CRM (sales pipeline, contacts, deals, sequences, integrations) and Afri ERP (books, invoicing, inventory, e-invoicing compliance). Planned additions include Afri Helpdesk, Afri Projects, and Afri Marketing — all connected to the same workspace and data layer.

For more information, visit afri-crm-production.up.railway.app or contact press@afri-crm-production.up.railway.app.
