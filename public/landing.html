Why teams choose Afri OS

Built different, on purpose

Every feature here exists because we built it for African businesses first — not as an afterthought to a Western template.

Sign up with Google

One click. No password to remember, no email verification loop. New users land in the CRM in 5 seconds with their calendar auto-connected.

Calendar auto-pulled

The moment you sign up with Google, today's meetings show on your home dashboard. No separate "Connect Calendar" step — it just works.

Beautiful by default

Modern glass-morphism UI, smooth animations, dark mode, mobile-first. The wizard alone has a 4-step transition that feels premium.

Mobile responsive

Installable as a PWA. Drag deals on touch. Bottom-nav on mobile. Designed for the phone-first reality of African business.

Privacy-first

Owner-only permanent account deletion in Settings → Danger Zone. One click, all data gone. No 30-day grace period nonsense.

African-first

Default Naira, Lagos time, NDPR-aware. Built in Nigeria, for Nigeria first — and the rest of the continent second.

Lead scoring built-in

Weights adapt to your industry, channel, and goal. Hot leads bubble up; cold ones get queued for later. No "score 1-100" guesswork — Afri does it for you.

Sequences & workflows

Trigger-based automations: when a deal moves to "Negotiation", auto-email the buyer a quote. When a contact goes cold for 7 days, ping the owner. No-code, visual.

Command palette (⌘K)

Jump anywhere in the app in 2 keystrokes. Create a contact, jump to a deal, switch to dark mode, search records — all from one keyboard-first launcher.
