Afri OS · Our story

We're building the software Africa deserves

Not a Western template localized for Nigeria. A platform built from the ground up for the way African businesses actually work — fast, mobile-first, and connected to the apps people already use.

Why we exist

The problem we couldn't ignore

African businesses pay $50–$300 per seat per month for CRM software designed for enterprise sales teams in San Francisco. They don't have WhatsApp integrations. They don't understand phone-first lead flow. They require 4-week onboarding with a consultant.

That's broken. Most African businesses run on WhatsApp, phone calls, and spreadsheets. When they try a CRM, they get a tool that asks them to fill in 12 mandatory fields just to add a contact, requires IMAP server configuration to connect email, and ships with lead scoring weights calibrated for B2B SaaS sales cycles in California.

We started Afri OS in 2026 because we kept watching founders pick between two bad options: pay HubSpot prices for software that doesn't fit, or stay on Excel and lose the deal when they hit 200 contacts.

Software shouldn't be a tax on African businesses. It should feel like it was made for them — because it was. — The Afri OS founding team

So we built the opposite. Afri CRM lets you sign up with Google in one click. Your calendar auto-connects. Your inbox connects with another click. The pipeline stages are pre-configured for Nigerian sales motion. The default currency is Naira. The support email is on Lagos time. And it's free forever — because the alternative is that founders stay on spreadsheets.

Afri ERP came next — same philosophy, applied to the operational side: books, invoicing, inventory, e-invoicing compliance. And we're building Afri Helpdesk, Projects, and Marketing on the same foundation. Same workspace, same sign-in, same data layer. No copying. No sync errors. One African operating system for business.

What we believe

Our values

The five principles that shape every product decision.

African-first, always

Default Naira. Lagos time. NDPR-aware. Phone-first. We build for Nigeria first and the continent second — never the other way around.

Fast beats perfect

Sign up in 5 seconds. Connect Gmail in 1 click. We optimize for time-to-value, not feature count. A feature ships when it's useful — not when it's polished.

Privacy is the default

Owner-only account deletion in one click. No 30-day grace period nonsense. We don't sell data, train models on it, or share it. Your data is yours.

Mobile is the main screen

70% of African web traffic is on mobile. We design for the phone first, then scale up. Installable PWA. Drag deals on touch. Bottom nav.

Open by design

API keys, webhooks, SDKs, MCP. We don't lock you in. If you want to leave, export your data and leave. We'd rather earn your stay.

Free forever for builders

Free tier generous enough for a solo founder to run a real business on it. We make money when you scale — not when you start.
