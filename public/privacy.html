Privacy Policy

How Afri CRM collects, uses, and protects your data. Plain language, no surprises.

Effective: October 19, 2026 Owner: Afri CRM Contact: privacy@afri-crm-production.up.railway.app

This Privacy Policy describes how Afri CRM ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our customer relationship management platform (the "Service"). By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (hashed with bcrypt) when you sign up using email/password. If you sign up using Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google.
  • Workspace information — your business name, industry, sales channel, and primary goal that you provide during the onboarding wizard.
  • CRM records you create — contacts, companies, deals, activities, notes, tasks, and any other records you add to your workspace.
  • Optional feedback — if you choose to provide a reason when permanently deleting your workspace, we log that feedback to improve the product.

1.2 Information from connected services

When you (or your workspace owner, in the case of platform OAuth) connect a third-party service like Google or Microsoft, we receive and store the OAuth access and refresh tokens that service returns. We use these tokens only to make API calls you explicitly authorize — for example, fetching today's calendar events or sending emails on your behalf from the CRM.

2. Google API Data Use & Disclosure

Afri CRM uses Google APIs to provide core CRM functionality. The scopes we request are limited to what is strictly necessary for the features you use:

ScopeWhat it lets us doUsed for
openid Verify your identity via Google Sign-In Account creation and sign-in
email Read your Google account email address Account creation, sign-in, contact email
profile Read your name and profile picture Account creation, displaying your name in the CRM
calendar.readonly See and download your primary Google Calendar events Display today's meetings on the home dashboard (auto-connected during Google SSO signup)
gmail.send Send emails on your behalf through your Gmail account Send emails from contact records and sequences
gmail.readonly Read your Gmail messages and labels Display email history for a contact on their record page
We do NOT:
  • Use your Google data for advertising purposes
  • Sell your Google data to third parties
  • Share your Google data with third parties except as required by law or to provide the Service
  • Use your Google data to train machine learning models
  • Transfer your Google data to any other application or service

All Google API scopes Afri CRM requests comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We access Google data solely to provide the features you explicitly enabled.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service's features (CRM records, pipelines, automations, integrations)
  • To authenticate you and secure your account
  • To send you service-related emails (account confirmation, security alerts, billing)
  • To display your connected calendar and email data inside the CRM (only when you have explicitly connected those services)
  • To analyze and improve product features (using aggregated, non-personal data)
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your data. We share data only in these limited cases:

  • With infrastructure providers — Railway (hosting), Google Cloud (OAuth provider), Cloudflare (DNS/CDN). These providers process data on our behalf under their own data protection agreements.
  • With your team members — when you invite users to your workspace, they can see CRM records that you share with them. Workspace owners can manage team member access at any time from Settings → Team.
  • To comply with legal obligations — if required by law, court order, or government request, we may disclose information necessary to comply.
  • With your consent — for any other sharing, we ask for your explicit consent first.

5. Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (no plaintext passwords stored)
  • JWT tokens expire after 8 hours; refresh requires re-authentication
  • OAuth tokens are stored in the database and only used for API calls you explicitly authorize
  • HTTPS enforced on all connections
  • Content Security Policy headers restrict script and style sources
  • Workspace owners can permanently delete all data at any time from Settings → Workspace → Danger Zone

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your workspace is active. When a workspace owner permanently deletes the workspace (Settings → Workspace → Danger Zone), we hard-delete all data within 24 hours — including:

  • All CRM records (contacts, companies, deals, activities, notes, emails, pipelines, tags, automations)
  • All user accounts and sessions for that workspace
  • All OAuth tokens for that workspace
  • All API keys and webhooks for that workspace
  • The workspace itself

We do not maintain backups of deleted workspace data — deletion is permanent and irreversible.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — sign in and view your CRM records at any time
  • Export your data — via the Integrations page API or by exporting individual record types
  • Delete your data — Settings → Workspace → Danger Zone → Permanently delete workspace
  • Revoke OAuth access — disconnect Gmail, Calendar, or Outlook at any time from the Integrations page; revoke Afri CRM's access entirely from your Google Account settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Object to processing — for legitimate reasons, contact us at privacy@afri-crm-production.up.railway.app

8. International Data Transfers

Afri CRM is hosted on Railway, with infrastructure primarily in the United States and Europe. Your data may be processed in these regions. By using the Service, you consent to these transfers.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the relevant age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at privacy@afri-crm-production.up.railway.app and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting a notice in the CRM or sending you an email. The "Effective" date above indicates when this version was last updated.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@afri-crm-production.up.railway.app
  • Workspace owner portal: Sign in → Settings → Workspace
