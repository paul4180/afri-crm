How we work

The culture, in practice

We don't hire people who need to be told what to do. We hire people who can be told what the goal is — and they figure out how to get there.

Write things down. Decisions live in documents, not Slack threads. Every feature ships with a public changelog entry. Every incident ships with a postmortem.

Async by default. No standups. No "quick calls". If a meeting can be a doc, it's a doc. The few meetings we have are 25 minutes, end with action items, and the host is responsible for the write-up.

Ship on Friday. We don't gate releases with QA cycles or staging environments. If you can ship it, ship it. If it breaks, fix it in public — the blog is your staging.

African context first. We design for mobile. We default to Naira. We test on MTN and Glo networks. We don't ship features that assume fiber internet.
