Docs

Afri CRM is the sales product inside Afri. You use it in the browser. There is no separate app to install.

Start

  1. Open Sign in and create a workspace with your business name.
  2. Answer the setup, or leave it. It asks what you sell, your goal, and which mail you use.
  3. Add a contact, then a deal. The home page orders work by what is overdue.

What you can do

  • Keep contacts, companies, deals, tasks, and a pipeline.
  • Connect Gmail and Google Calendar from the account menu.
  • Search with the box at the top, or with ⌘K.
  • Remove the practice records from Sample data. They are marked [Sample].

What is not built yet

  • A paid plan, invoices for Afri itself, and a spend cap.
  • A WhatsApp number that belongs to your workspace.
  • A published OpenAPI file. The route list is on the API page.
